## Vignette

At the Holo Bazaar, good brokers teach a pause before the try-on.

Not the purchase. The try-on. A neural sample, a mood scarf, a minute of someone else’s courage for sale. The stall lights wait. The broker waits. The shopper’s agent may already be eager; that is not consent.

Regulars call it the consent pause. Two breaths. A yes that is spoken, tapped, or sung into the Trust Loom stub at the stall edge. No means the hologram collapses politely and the price tag stops performing.

Tourists who skip it get a soft lockout and a lesson: the Bazaar sells sensation, not ambush. Vendors who skip it lose stall privileges faster than they lose inventory.

A clerk’s summary for apprentices is almost boring. “Pause. Hear yes. Then open the sample.”
