## Scene

Reya did not join the dock net. She answered the council thread where the stamp already lived.

Subject: *Nursery edge — proposed wider-cut lane (habitat log addendum).* Body short enough to survive a skim: fisher objection noted; feeding people is not the dispute; the dispute is cutting the waypoint edge. Attached: a simple overlay of the stamped turbidity line plus a suggested surface lane bent wider of the sung mark. No mood layer. No live mirror. No bazaar path.

“I’m giving them a door that isn’t the nursery,” she told Arin.

“Good,” he said from under the lattice. “I’d rather argue about a line on a map than a shelf full of children-of-coral.”

A fisher rep liked the lane in principle and asked whether the watermark could be removed “for morale.” Reya’s reply was one clause: *Watermark stays; lane is the courtesy.*

She filed the addendum beside yesterday’s tape. Live feed dark. Boundary held with a pencil, not a speech.
