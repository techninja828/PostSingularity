## Vignette

In the Submerged Archives, stewards teach visitors to ask narrow.

The halls are twilight on purpose. Pressure seals hum. A wide request — “show me everything about Day 0” — unlocks spectacle and argument. A narrow request — “show me one coastal weather log from the decade before” — unlocks a room you can actually leave.

AI archivists will happily expand a query until the ocean of memory feels like drowning. Guests who want to prove a theory in an afternoon get politely slowed. The Archives are not a courtroom snack bar.

The line apprentices copy into their journals is almost stern. “Ask narrow. Read fully. Expand only with a reason.”

Outside the elevator, the surface world can stay loud. Down here, curiosity keeps a smaller mouth.
