## Vignette

Visitors sometimes ask Toma to let a neural scan “save” a bowl so it can be replicated elsewhere.

He finishes the cut he is already making before he answers. Sawdust settles. The bowl stays itself.

“You can watch,” he says. “You can learn. You cannot photocopy the patience.”

Analog Haven already dampens the mesh; that is not his whole point. Even if a scan were perfect, the enclave’s rule is older than the dampeners: craft that arrives without struggle arrives as decoration. He will trade a finished piece. He will not authorize a ghost of his hands.

Apprentices get a shorter version. “No scan. Your hands are the archive.”

Then he turns the bowl to the next stubborn grain and keeps working like the question was weather.
