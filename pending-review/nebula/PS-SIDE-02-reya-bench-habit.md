## Vignette

Reya puts gloves on before she opens a link.

It looks like superstition to visiting techs who live in overlay-first workflows. To her it is sequencing. Hands meet the object. Then the slate. Then, if the numbers still argue with her skin, the neural channel.

She learned the habit in the Submerged Archives, reading schematics that had outlived their authors. A beautiful overlay can lie with confidence. Gloves cannot invent a stress line that is not there; they only slow you down enough to notice when one is.

Apprentices get the rule without a speech. “Gloves first. If the bench still surprises you after that, then you earn the link.”

Her harness stays unlatched at the shoulders while she works the first pass. Ready to move. Not ready to float away into a simulation of the work.
