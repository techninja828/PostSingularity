## Vignette

On the Sanctuary’s observation deck, voices go soft before the glass does.

Caretaker AIs keep life support invisible on purpose. Residents keep chatter invisible on purpose too. The rule is not silence forever. It is a quiet deck hour when Earth is in frame and nobody pitches a project into the view.

Newcomers learn by getting gently redirected: take the logistics to a corridor node; leave the window for looking. Someone always tries to open a shared feed of the planet as entertainment. Hosts dim it. Looking is not content here.

A steward’s line for guests is short enough to memorize. “Quiet deck. Breathe with the curve. Talk later.”

When the hour ends, the hum of ventilation feels louder for a minute, and that is considered normal.
