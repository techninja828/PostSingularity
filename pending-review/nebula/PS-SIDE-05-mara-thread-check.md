## Vignette

Before Mara opens a dispute circle, she checks the Memory Thread the way some people check the weather.

Not to decide the outcome. To hear which feelings already have names and which ones are still weather without a map. She reads origin points, resonance marks, and whether anyone has tried to retire the story early.

Visitors sometimes want her to skip straight to ritual. She shakes her head once, beads soft against her shoulder. “If we sing before we know the thread, we only decorate the misunderstanding.”

AI sentiment can sit in the room. Final wording stays human. When the thread is thin, she slows the circle. When it is loud, she asks for one quieter witness first.

Apprentices get the short form. “Check the thread. Then talk.”
