## Vignette

At Analog Haven’s gate, visitors hang the link like a coat.

There is a row of hooks under the eaves. Some people leave a neural clip. Some leave a wrist agent. Everyone leaves the habit of being pingable. Inside the dampeners, the mesh is already quiet; the hooks make the choice visible.

Hosts hand over a paper journal without ceremony. “If it matters, write it. If it can wait, it can wait.” Children learn faster than adults. Adults keep reaching for a pocket that no longer answers.

Leaving is the reverse ritual: reclaim the coat, step past the ash line, let the second channel warm up slowly. Regulars say the Haven works best when you do not sprint back into the feed.

The short form for first-timers is almost a joke. “Hook first. Words second. Mesh later.”
