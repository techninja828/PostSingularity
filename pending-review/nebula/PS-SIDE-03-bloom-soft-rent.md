## Beat

**Soft rent** — oceanic slang for what a nursery shelf is owed when people take data, shade, or passage near it.

Not currency. Not a bazaar SKU. A spoken ledger: you paid soft rent if you slowed your wake, logged without scraping live mood, or left the shelf darker than you found it. You skipped soft rent if you treated the nursery like free ambience.

Runners teach it as manners before they teach it as policy. “Pay soft rent or don’t visit.” Elders shrug when outsiders ask for a conversion rate. The point is the pause, not the invoice.
