## Vignette

Kai mutes their agent before the hard part of a conversation.

Not forever. Not as theatre. As a choice to hear one room instead of two. The agent can return with logistics after the human heat has a place to land.

They learned it in orbital habitats where every voice had a schedule and empathy still had to fit. An agent that summarizes too early turns a dispute into a checklist. Kai would rather be briefly less efficient than accidentally less present.

Partners sometimes notice the mute icon and smile. Kai shrugs. “One channel. Then we can afford the second.”

When the mute ends, they do not ask the agent who was right. They ask what still needs arranging.
