### AI fusion-control framework tested in five real experiments

- Event date: 2026-09-02
- Sources: `S1`
- Observed fact: Researchers at Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory and Princeton University announced PACMAN, a framework that allows multiple AI models to read plasma measurements and issue control decisions. PPPL reported that the system was successfully tested on a real fusion system in five experiments and can make decisions on millisecond timescales, faster than human operators can respond. The framework keeps human operators responsible for high-level goals and safety constraints.
- Significance: This is a concrete enabling development for fusion operations rather than a claim of net-energy production. Fast automated control could help manage unstable plasma states and reduce the operational burden of future fusion devices. It also links the fusion and AI signals: increasingly autonomous control may be necessary for machines whose relevant dynamics occur faster than human intervention.

### Tokamak Energy reports high-temperature-superconducting magnet endurance results

- Event date: 2026-09-09
- Sources: `S3`
- Observed fact: Tokamak Energy reported completion of a 14-month campaign on its Demo4 high-temperature-superconducting magnet system. The company reported a peak magnetic field of 13.7 tesla, operation of toroidal-field coils at 5,600 amps, cycling between 77 kelvin and 17 kelvin, transverse mechanical stresses of 150 MPa, roughly 10,000 hours of operation in various energized states, multiple forced discharges up to 12.5 tesla, and monitoring through more than 600 sensors.
- Significance: High-field superconducting magnets are a major enabling technology for compact tokamak concepts because stronger magnetic fields can support higher plasma pressure and potentially smaller devices. The reported endurance and fault-testing figures are more material than a generic fusion roadmap announcement, and the work is tied to the United Kingdom’s STEP program. If independently validated and transferred to reactor-scale systems, this could reduce some engineering barriers to fusion plants.

- Event date: 2026-09-09
