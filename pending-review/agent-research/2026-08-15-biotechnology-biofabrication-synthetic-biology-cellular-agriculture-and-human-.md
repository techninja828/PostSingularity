## Watchlist

- Industrial biomanufacturing demonstrations with audited feedstock, energy, downstream-processing, labor, and total-cost data.
- Cultivated-meat production crossing intermediate bioreactor scales, especially evidence addressing the 20-2000 liter gap and biological validation.
- Closed-loop material recovery and autonomous factories demonstrating local production from common feedstocks rather than laboratory-scale proof of concept.
- Regulatory authorization and monitoring decisions for cultivated meat, new genomic techniques, synthetic biology, and AI-enabled biological design.
- Adoption of mandatory AI provenance, model-audit, disclosure, screening, and incident-reporting standards.
- Independent results from AI-biology security and safety research, including deployed assurance systems rather than workshop announcements.
- Long-term implantable BCI studies reporting channel capacity, bidirectionality, recalibration frequency, safety, privacy, and performance outside specialist clinical settings.
- Clinical-trial registries and conference results for neural interfaces, including evidence involving sensory decoding, affective signals, or healthy-user enhancement.
- Closed-loop life-support performance, human-health data, launch economics, in-space manufacturing, and autonomous operations for long-duration orbital or off-world communities.
- Demonstrations of reliable closed-loop neurostimulation or immersive mental-state control, together with evidence of consent safeguards, dependency, adoption, and neuro-rights governance.
