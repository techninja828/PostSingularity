### Cell-free biosynthesis produced raspberry ketone and dobutamine through artificial enzymatic pathways

- Event date: 2026-09-25
- Sources: `S1`
- Observed fact: A ShanghaiTech University team reported an artificial, retrosynthesis-guided cell-free pathway that produced raspberry ketone at 1.33 mM with 66% conversion yield. The system also produced dobutamine through an engineered reductive-amination cascade; protein engineering and byproduct recycling increased dobutamine production by approximately 10^4-fold relative to the wild-type enzyme. Preparative-scale synthesis isolated 19.2 mg of raspberry ketone, and both modules remained functional when scaled from microliter to milliliter volumes.
- Significance: This is a concrete step toward manufacturing selected chemicals and pharmaceutical intermediates without maintaining living production organisms. It is relevant to PS-MATTER-001 because it demonstrates production from defined biochemical components, but the scale and product range remain far from materially reducing physical scarcity.

- Sources: `S2`

- Significance: The approval is a regulatory milestone for targeted biological intervention in a disease affecting neurodevelopment and endocrine function.
