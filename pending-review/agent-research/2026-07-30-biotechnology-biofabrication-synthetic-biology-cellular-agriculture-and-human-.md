### Engineered microbial polyketide synthases produced tunable monomers for recyclable plastics

- Event date: 2026-07-27
- Sources: `S3`
- Observed fact: A Nature Biotechnology article published July 27, 2026 reported engineered type I polyketide synthases in Escherichia coli and Streptomyces hosts that biosynthesized beta-keto-delta-lactone monomers for polydiketoenamine plastics. The study screened 144 candidate monomers, achieved a reported bioreactor titer of 1.84 grams per liter for one product, produced polymers with glass-transition temperatures from 53 to 98 degrees Celsius, and demonstrated temperature-gated depolymerization across approximately 0–60 degrees Celsius. The authors also reported technoeconomic and life-cycle analyses indicating that corn-stover-derived monomers could outperform petrochemical dimedone on modeled cost and greenhouse-gas emissions.
- Significance: This is one of the clearest window-period examples of synthetic biology extending beyond molecules into programmable, recoverable physical materials. It supports the material-scarcity signal in a qualified way: biology can supply tunable feedstocks and closed-loop material chemistries, but the work remains an early biomanufacturing platform rather than proof of low-cost, distributed fabrication.

### Enantioselective biosensors compressed a biocatalyst-screening workflow from days to minutes

- Event date: 2026-07-23
- Sources: `S4`
- Observed fact: A Nature Chemical Biology article published July 23, 2026 described engineered enantioselective transcription-factor biosensors that convert chiral reaction products into fluorescent signals. The researchers screened more than 300,000 transcription-factor variants using massively parallel reporter assays, evolved sensors with 51-fold and 16-fold enantiomeric selectivity, and used fluorescence-activated cell sorting to screen a library of approximately 8,000 imine reductases in about 30 minutes. The authors contrasted this with a chiral LC–MS workflow that would require approximately 11 days and 16 liters of solvent for the comparable screen.
- Significance: This is a measurable acceleration of the design-build-test-learn loop in synthetic biology. - Cross-laboratory validation and regulatory acceptance of AI-enabled bioprinting and biofabrication systems.
