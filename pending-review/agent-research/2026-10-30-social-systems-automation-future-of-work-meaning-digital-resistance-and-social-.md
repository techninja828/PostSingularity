## Executive Summary

The audited evidence shows institutional adaptation to AI becoming more explicit, but not a completed transition to post-work abundance. Governments and labor institutions are focusing on employment continuity, displacement risk, reskilling, income security, worker rights, care work, autonomy, and oversight. AI adoption is also reallocating some technical work toward judgment, architecture, evaluation, and override authority. These developments make a partial shift toward care, human judgment, and meaning plausible, while leaving material insecurity and compulsory employment unresolved. Evidence for analog revival and digital resistance is indirect and insufficient to establish a broad social movement. No material evidence updates the assumptions concerning high-bandwidth neural interfaces or sustained off-world settlement. Canon review should therefore qualify the social timeline and strengthen the labor-governance dimension of Trust Fabrics, while leaving the analog, neural-link, and aerospace files unchanged pending stronger evidence.
