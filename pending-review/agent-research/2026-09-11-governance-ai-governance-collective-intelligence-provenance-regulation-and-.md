## Executive Summary

The evidence materially strengthens the view that AI influence is driving formal audit, assurance, transparency, and governance infrastructure, including enacted California audit legislation and expanded European and international frameworks. It simultaneously limits optimistic conclusions: major control failures, insufficient audit access, provenance fragility, incomplete incident reporting, implementation burdens, and public reluctance to delegate authority remain evident. Formal oversight is not equivalent to effective accountability, and transparency does not reliably produce trust. Community-assembly and collective-intelligence initiatives remain agenda-setting rather than demonstrated governance institutions.
