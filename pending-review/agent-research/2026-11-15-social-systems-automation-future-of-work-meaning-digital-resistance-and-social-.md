AI forecasts point to broad workforce adaptation, while current surveys and reporting show modest gains, incomplete role redesign, skills gaps, organizational resistance, liability concerns, and limited conversion of freed time into innovative or meaningful activity. The supplied record does not validate basic-income effects, reduced compulsory working hours, measurable shifts toward care or creativity, sustained analog revival, or off-world settlement. ### PS-AI-003: AI influence drives stronger provenance and audit systems

- PostSingularity implication: A post-singularity society would likely need provenance and graduated oversight as social institutions, not merely technical metadata. Trust would depend on interoperable, inspectable, and contestable records plus credible fallback procedures; no single watermark, detector, or credential should be assumed to establish authenticity or accountability.

The measures differ between positive returns and desired cost savings or time redirection, so they should not be treated as a direct numerical contradiction.

## Watchlist

- Working-hour, wage, and time-use data showing whether automation reduces compulsory labor or merely changes tasks and supervision.
- Basic-income, social-protection, and productivity-sharing experiments that convert automation gains into secure time or income.
- Worker-reported autonomy, role clarity, contribution, psychological safety, care participation, and creative participation after AI deployment.
- Adoption and enforcement of device-free spaces, right-to-disconnect rules, analog-media practices, and low-technology communities.
- Legislative progress and implementation data for the Digital Fairness Act, including opt-out use and access to human contact.
- Independent measurements of Content Credentials coverage, retention through platform distribution, user comprehension, and attribution reliability.
- Adopted interoperable provenance and AI-content taxonomies, plus evidence that layered audit systems improve trust or reduce manipulation.
- Real-world deepfake detection and watermarking performance across platforms, modalities, recompression, editing, and open-weight models.
- Prospective multicenter neural-interface studies reporting long-term safety, bidirectional bandwidth, decoded speech or affect, privacy outcomes, and end-of-use procedures.
- Launch costs, orbital duration, life-support closure rates, in-space manufacturing, autonomous maintenance, radiation mitigation, and long-duration human-health outcomes.
