- Event date: 2026-08-11 through 2026-08-18; underlying legal application date: 2026-08-02
- Sources: `S1`, `S2`, `S3`
- Observed fact: From 2 August 2026, Article 50 transparency obligations apply to providers and deployers of covered generative-AI systems.
